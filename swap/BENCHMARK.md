Delta is Xark minus gnark, with the percentage of the gnark value. The shielded-pool program differs between the implementations, so `SPP CPI CU` is not an apples-to-apples comparison; `Verify CU` and the circuit metrics are. Timings from runs on different machines or under different load do not compare.
